2. Data within the app

Schrottarena stores game progress and settings on your device: active and reserve robots, robot levels, equipment and module levels, formation, scrap, area and wave, battle results, tutorial progress, language and sound settings. The app also stores a Google Play purchase receipt for the full-game unlock, as described in section 3. A local backup helps recover a corrupted save.

This data allows you to resume the game and is not transmitted to us by the app. Schrottarena requires no separate Schrottarena account. It includes no advertising or separately integrated analytics or crash-reporting services. The optional full-game unlock involves purchase data as described in section 3. It does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Operating-system backups are separate from the app's local storage. Depending on your Android version and device settings, local app data, including settings and purchase receipts, may be backed up or transferred to another device through Android backup or device-transfer features. These features are provided by the operating system and the relevant backup provider; they do not give us access to your backups.

To the extent this local information constitutes personal data, it is processed to provide the game features you use, based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3. One-time full-game unlock through Google Play

The free introduction includes the tutorial, all six robots and the Scrapyard with five waves including its boss. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the remaining four worlds – Foundry, Cooling Plant, Reactor Core and Iron Fortress – and their available module upgrades. This is a non-consumable unlock, not a subscription. The applicable price is shown by Google Play before purchase.

Google Play handles payment using your Google Play account and the payment methods it offers. The billing integration does not provide the app with your full card or bank details.

To unlock content, acknowledge purchases and check entitlements, the billing integration processes the product ID, purchase state and a technical proof of purchase (purchase token). Depending on the transaction, an order ID and purchase time may also be available. This data is used to provide the purchased content and account for changes to purchase entitlements. The app stores the receipt supplied by Google Play, including its purchase token, receipt data and digital signature, in local app storage and verifies the signature on the device. It does not send the receipt to a developer-operated verification server; no such server is used.

Where we process personal data to fulfil the purchase and provide access, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. The paid unlock cannot be provided without the necessary purchase verification; the free introduction remains available.

The app queries Google Play for product information and existing purchase entitlements, including at launch, when returning to the app and when selecting “Restore purchase”. Purchases and acknowledgements are also handled through Google Play. Purchasing and restoring require an internet connection. A valid unlock can be restored using the same Google Play account; this does not restore a deleted game save. A previously verified local receipt allows unlocked content to be used without a connection. A confirmed loss of entitlement causes the local purchase receipt to be removed.

For its store and payment services, Google processes account, transaction and payment information as well as technical device and connection data. Details of the relevant Google entities, recipients, international transfers, safeguards and deletion options are provided in Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice applicable to your payments profile.

Where Google Play makes transaction information available to us through order management, or you send us a receipt for a support enquiry, we use it to handle the purchase, refunds and purchase-related enquiries and to meet legal obligations.

4. No advertising or additional purchase offers

This version includes no advertisements, rewarded videos or advertising SDKs. It offers no subscription, real-money scrap purchases or paid retries. The free Scrapyard remains replayable. Cosmetic packs are not part of this version.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it. On Android, use the system settings under “Apps → Schrottarena → Storage → Clear data”; labels may vary by device. This removes local progress, settings and the locally stored purchase receipt. “New run” replaces the previous run but does not erase the purchase receipt or all settings. Manage any operating-system backups separately with the relevant provider.

Deleting local app data does not delete the Google Play transaction or trigger a refund. Entitlement information is needed for as long as required to provide and restore access. Google’s privacy and payment notices and applicable legal retention duties govern its transaction records.

Local data is stored in the storage provided for the app by Android. Purchase entitlements are accessed through the Google Play billing integration; full payment details are not stored in the app. Personal data we retain for support or order handling is deleted when no longer needed for these purposes, unless legal retention duties or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims require further retention.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address, any name you provide and your message to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual enquiries, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries. Our legitimate interest is answering questions about the game.

We use Google Gmail for the contact address. Google describes its processing, including outside the European Economic Area, and the relevant safeguards in its Privacy Policy. Contact enquiries are retained only as long as needed to handle them or meet legal retention duties. Contacting us is voluntary; without a return address we cannot reply.

8. Your rights

Where the legal conditions apply, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where consent applies, you may withdraw it for the future.
