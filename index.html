Schrottarena speichert Spielstand und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät: Team und Reserve, Roboterlevel, Ausrüstung und Modulstufen, Aufstellung, Schrott, Gebiet und Welle, Kampfergebnis, Tutorialfortschritt sowie Sprach- und Toneinstellungen. Sie enthält keine Werbung und keine zusätzlich eingebundenen Analyse- oder Crash-Reporting-Dienste. Für die optionale Vollversionsfreischaltung werden Kaufdaten verarbeitet, wie in Abschnitt 3 beschrieben. Sie greift nicht auf Kontakte, Standort, Kamera oder Mikrofon zu.

3. Einmalige Vollversionsfreischaltung über Google Play

Der kostenlose Einstieg umfasst das Tutorial, alle sechs Roboter und den Schrottplatz mit fünf Wellen einschließlich Boss. Die weiteren vier Welten – Gießerei, Kühlwerk, Reaktorkern und Eisenfestung – samt den dort verfügbaren Modul-Upgrades werden durch einen einmaligen In-App-Kauf freigeschaltet. Es handelt sich um eine nicht verbrauchbare Freischaltung, nicht um ein Abonnement. Maßgeblich ist der vor dem Kauf in Google Play angezeigte Preis.

Die Zahlung erfolgt über Google Play. Dazu nutzt du dein Google-Play-Konto und die dort angebotenen Zahlungsmethoden. Vollständige Kreditkarten- oder Bankdaten werden der App durch die Kaufanbindung nicht übermittelt.

Zur Freischaltung, Bestätigung und Prüfung der Kaufberechtigung verarbeitet die Kaufanbindung insbesondere die Produktkennung, den Kaufstatus und einen technischen Kaufnachweis (Kauf-Token). Je nach Kaufvorgang stehen außerdem Bestellkennung und Kaufzeitpunkt zur Verfügung. Diese Daten dienen dazu, den gekauften Inhalt bereitzustellen und Änderungen der Kaufberechtigung zu berücksichtigen.

Soweit wir personenbezogene Daten zur Abwicklung des Kaufs und Bereitstellung der Freischaltung verarbeiten, erfolgt dies zur Vertragserfüllung gemäß Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO. Eine gesetzlich vorgeschriebene Aufbewahrung erfolgt gemäß Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. c DSGVO. Ohne die erforderliche Kaufprüfung kann die kostenpflichtige Freischaltung nicht bereitgestellt werden; der kostenlose Einstieg bleibt nutzbar.

Für die Wiederherstellung einer gültigen Freischaltung ist eine erneute Abfrage der Kaufberechtigung über Google Play mit demselben Google-Konto vorgesehen. Dafür ist eine Internetverbindung erforderlich. Die Wiederherstellung betrifft die Freischaltung; sie stellt keinen gelöschten Spielstand wieder her.

Google verarbeitet für seine Store- und Zahlungsdienste unter anderem Konto-, Transaktions- und Zahlungsinformationen sowie technische Geräte- und Verbindungsdaten. Schrottarena Privacy Policy

Draft dated 6 October 2026 · Covers the Android app and this information page.

AdiStar666
Email: r83.adolf@gmail.com

2. Data within the app

Schrottarena stores game progress and settings on your device: active and reserve robots, robot levels, equipment and module levels, formation, scrap, area and wave, battle results, tutorial progress, language and sound settings. The full-game unlock status is also stored locally. A local backup helps recover a corrupted save.

This data allows you to resume the game and is not transmitted to us by the app. The version described here requires no separate Schrottarena account. It includes no advertising or separately integrated analytics or crash-reporting services. The optional full-game unlock involves purchase data as described in section 3. It does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Operating-system backups are separate from the app's local storage. Whether app data is included depends on app configuration and your device settings.

3. One-time full-game unlock through Google Play

The free introduction includes the tutorial, all six robots and the Scrapyard with five waves including its boss. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the remaining four worlds – Foundry, Cooling Plant, Reactor Core and Iron Fortress – and their available module upgrades. This is a non-consumable unlock, not a subscription. The applicable price is shown by Google Play before purchase.

Google Play handles payment using your Google Play account and the payment methods it offers. The billing integration does not provide the app with your full card or bank details.

To unlock content, acknowledge purchases and check entitlements, the billing integration processes the product ID, purchase state and a technical proof of purchase (purchase token). Depending on the transaction, an order ID and purchase time may also be available. This data is used to provide the purchased content and account for changes to purchase entitlements.

Where we process personal data to fulfil the purchase and provide access, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. The paid unlock cannot be provided without the necessary purchase verification; the free introduction remains available.

Restoring a valid unlock is intended to involve querying Google Play again with the same Google account. An internet connection is required. This restores the entitlement, not a deleted game save.

For its store and payment services, Google processes account, transaction and payment information as well as technical device and connection data. Details of the relevant Google entities, recipients, international transfers, safeguards and deletion options are provided in Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice applicable to your payments profile.

4. No advertising or additional purchase offers

This version includes no advertisements, rewarded videos or advertising SDKs. It offers no subscription, real-money scrap purchases or paid retries. The free Scrapyard remains replayable. Cosmetic packs and possible future advertising models are not part of the version described here.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it. On Android, use the system settings under “Apps → Schrottarena → Storage → Clear data”; labels may vary by device. This removes local progress and settings. “New run” replaces the previous run but does not erase all settings. Manage any operating-system backups separately with the relevant provider.

Deleting local app data does not delete the Google Play transaction or trigger a refund. Entitlement information is needed for as long as required to provide and restore access. Google’s privacy and payment notices and applicable legal retention duties govern its transaction records.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address, any name you provide and your message to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual enquiries, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries. Our legitimate interest is answering questions about the game.

We use Google Gmail for the contact address. Google describes its processing, including outside the European Economic Area, and the relevant safeguards in its Privacy Policy. Contact enquiries are retained only as long as needed to handle them or meet legal retention duties. Contacting us is voluntary; without a return address we cannot reply.

7. Visiting this GitHub Pages website

This information page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical connection data. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. We use hosting to provide this privacy policy securely, based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

GitHub explains international transfers, safeguards and retention criteria in its Privacy Statement. Its Pages documentation explains IP logging. This page itself includes no analytics, advertising services, remote fonts or embedded third-party content, and sets no cookies of its own. Language navigation uses section links without storing a preference.

8. Your rights

Where the legal conditions apply, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Where consent applies, you may withdraw it for the future. Contact us using the details above. We cannot access game saves stored only on your device.

You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the EU member state of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

9. Changes

We update this policy when features or processing change. Before introducing additional purchase offers, advertising or further online features, we will describe new processing and obtain any required consent.
