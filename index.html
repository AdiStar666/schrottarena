Schrottarena speichert Spielstand und Einstellungen auf deinem Gerät: Team und Reserve, Roboterlevel, Ausrüstung und Modulstufen, Aufstellung, Schrott, Gebiet und Welle, Kampfergebnis, Tutorialfortschritt sowie Sprach- und Toneinstellungen. 3. Speicherung und Löschen

Lokale Daten bleiben erhalten, bis du sie entfernst. Unter Android kannst du über die Systemeinstellungen unter „Apps → Schrottarena → Speicher → Daten löschen“ die lokalen App-Daten entfernen; die Bezeichnungen können je nach Gerät abweichen. Damit werden auch Fortschritt und Einstellungen gelöscht. „Neuer Durchlauf“ ersetzt den bisherigen Durchlauf, ist aber keine vollständige Löschung aller Einstellungen. Eventuelle Betriebssystem-Backups verwaltest du separat beim jeweiligen Anbieter.

2. Data within the app

Schrottarena stores game progress and settings on your device: active and reserve robots, robot levels, equipment and module levels, formation, scrap, area and wave, battle results, tutorial progress, language and sound settings. A local backup helps recover a corrupted save.

This data allows you to resume the game and is not transmitted to us by the app. The version described here requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting services or in-app purchases. It does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone.

VERIFY BEFORE PUBLICATION: Check release-version data flows, SDKs, permissions and Android backup configuration. Operating-system backups are separate from the app's local storage. Whether app data is included depends on app configuration and your device settings.

3. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it. On Android, use the system settings under “Apps → Schrottarena → Storage → Clear data”; labels may vary by device. This removes local progress and settings. “New run” replaces the previous run but does not erase all settings. Manage any operating-system backups separately with the relevant provider.

If you email us, we process your email address, any name you provide and your message to respond. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual enquiries, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries. Our legitimate interest is answering questions about the game.

We use Google Gmail for the contact address. Google describes its processing, including outside the European Economic Area, and the relevant safeguards in its Privacy Policy. Contact enquiries are retained only as long as needed to handle them or meet legal retention duties. Contacting us is voluntary; without a return address we cannot reply.

Where the legal conditions apply, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability. You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the EU member state of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
